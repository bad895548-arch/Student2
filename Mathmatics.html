📐 Mathematics Formulas

Square

Area = Side × Side

Perimeter = 4 × Side

Rectangle

Area = Length × Breadth

Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Breadth)

Triangle

Area = ½ × Base × Height

Perimeter = Sum of all sides

Circle

Area = π × Radius × Radius

Circumference = 2 × π × Radius

Percentage

Percentage = (Value ÷ Total) × 100

Speed

Speed = Distance ÷ Time
