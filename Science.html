Learn Science Step by Step

Chapter 1: Introduction to Science

What is Science?

Science is the study of the natural world. It helps us understand how things work through observation and experiments.

Importance of Science

  • Science helps us understand nature.
  • Science improves our daily life.
  • Science helps develop new technology.

Chapter 2: Matter

Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space.

Three States of Matter

  1. Solid – Ice, Stone, Wood
  2. Liquid – Water, Milk, Oil
  3. Gas – Oxygen, Carbon Dioxide

Chapter 3: Force and Motion

Force is a push or pull that can change the motion or shape of an object.

Example: Pushing a door or pulling a cart.

Chapter 4: Plants

Plants are living organisms. They make their own food through photosynthesis.

Chapter 5: Human Body

The human body has different organs. The heart pumps blood and the brain controls body activities.
