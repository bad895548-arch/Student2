Welcome to English Learning

English is an important language for communication. Good grammar helps us speak and write correctly. Regular practice improves our confidence and vocabulary.

1. English Grammar

Parts of Speech

There are eight main parts of speech in English.

  • Noun - Name of a person, place or thing.
  • Pronoun - Used instead of a noun.
  • Verb - Shows an action or state.
  • Adjective - Describes a noun.
  • Adverb - Describes a verb, adjective or another adverb.
  • Preposition - Shows relationship between words.
  • Conjunction - Joins words or sentences.
  • Interjection - Expresses sudden feelings.

Types of Tenses

  • Present Tense - I play cricket.
  • Past Tense - I played cricket.
  • Future Tense - I will play cricket.

Basic Sentence Structure

Subject + Verb + Object

Example: I read a book.

I = Subject, Read = Verb, A book = Object.

3. Speaking Skills

Daily Use English Sentences

  • Good morning. - Suprabhat.
  • How are you? - Aap kaise hain?
  • I am fine. - Main theek hoon.
  • What are you doing? - Aap kya kar rahe hain?
  • Thank you. - Dhanyavaad.
  • See you tomorrow. - Kal milte hain.

4. Writing Skills

How to Write a Good Paragraph

A paragraph is a group of sentences about one topic. Start with a main idea, explain it with details and finish with a concluding sentence.

Example: My School

My school is a beautiful place. It has many classrooms and a big playground. My teachers are kind and helpful. I enjoy learning new things at my school.
